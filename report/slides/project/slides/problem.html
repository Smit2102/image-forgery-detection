Two ways to fake a photograph

Copy-move

A region is duplicated inside the same image, to hide or repeat something.

Splicing

A region is pasted in from a different photograph.

Goal: decide whether an image was tampered with, and show where, using interpretable evidence.

Smit Patel · EDS 6364 · 2

Copy-move duplicates content within an image; splicing brings in content from another photo. The project goal from the proposal: classify authentic vs tampered and produce a map of the suspected region, with techniques whose evidence a person can inspect.
