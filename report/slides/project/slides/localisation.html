Localisation is the hardest part: tampered regions are small, with a median of 3% of the image. For regions above 5% the F1 is 0.333 under R. Copy-moves are localised better than splices (0.236 vs 0.129 under R). The ground truth marks only the pasted copy; on synthetic copy-moves the localiser was shown to mark both source and copy, which lowers F1 there.
